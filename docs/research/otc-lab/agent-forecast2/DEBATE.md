## Verdict

4 trades, 36 abstains. The dominant conflict is structural: on every stretched chart the continuation camp and the fade camp take opposite sides with near-identical confidence, which is what a panel looks like when neither reading has an edge. A trade needs >54.35% to pay, and no forecaster priced any case meaningfully above that. I took the four cases where both timeframes trend in order, the move is not at a z>2 extreme, and the last bar still pushes with the trend; each is a thin, low-conviction 0.55 call, all at 5 min (the horizon most forecasters used for these setups). Expect results near coin-flip; the abstains are the main protection.

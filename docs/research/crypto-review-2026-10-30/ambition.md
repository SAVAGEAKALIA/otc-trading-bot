# Ambition brief

Goal beyond the goal: decide whether a small systematic crypto operation deserves capital and can remain controllable when markets or venues fail.

Altitude: (1) correct the draft, (2) compare investable alternatives on total capital and evidence quality, (3) define a repeatable capital-allocation process that can reject every strategy and remain in an eligible external cash benchmark.

Depth: a professional starts with timestamped settlement records, execution records, collateral rules and account eligibility rather than headline APRs. Use deterministic reproduction, point-in-time data, portfolio accounting, pre-registered trials and adversarial incident replay. Hard target: zero material unqualified factual claims, no hindsight marketed as held-out evidence, and an explicit reason to fund or reject each sleeve. The outside judges are reproductions, primary sources and an independent critic with a frozen rubric.

Range: allocate, reconcile, survive and decide. The quantitative audit reproduces funding economics; the venue review checks jurisdiction and incident sources; the execution review applies recovery and distributed-system failure analysis. Separate agents perform these investigations. A cold critic judges the integrated proposal.

Access: draft, repository, local funding files and public primary sources are available. Nigeria residence confirmed by user. Capital and loss limits are configurable and unset. Authenticated eligibility, fee tier, bank/off-ramp path, untouched forward observations and live execution records are missing; none are invented.

Rewritten prompt:

```text
Improve the crypto proposal into a capital-allocation decision for a Nigerian resident. Reproduce the existing funding evidence from raw records, distinguish net notional yield from total-capital returns, verify material claims against primary sources, and compare no-build cash, gated funding carry, dated-futures basis and unlevered trend. Keep capital and loss budgets configurable with no permissive live defaults. Specify accounting, failure handling, approval boundaries and pre-registered promotion gates. Do the research now; do not trade, deploy or delete existing infrastructure. Pass only when deterministic checks reproduce, uncertain claims are labeled, and an independent critic finds no material unsupported or unsafe recommendation against the frozen rubric.
```

Highest rung completed by this work: a research-backed allocation proposal and validation contract. Actual strategy profitability remains unproven until the defined experiments and forward evidence exist.

# Venue, eligibility and failure-risk evidence review

Research date: 2026-10-30. Scope: public primary-source checks of the draft, not account-specific legal clearance. Nigeria residence was explicitly confirmed by Saviour during this review; no residency is inferred from the computer, timezone or server location. No account was opened or trade placed.

## Verdict

The draft's venue choice is premature. Its blanket EU claim, unconditional Nigeria availability implication, assumption that stablecoin earn is a safe idle sleeve, and promise that code caps tails should be removed. The decision must be about the exact legal entity, eligible product, settlement asset, custody structure and executable exit routes. A venue's public API responding does not establish any of those facts.

## Operational consequences

These are engineering conclusions from the failure modes, not claims that any chosen venue will fail.

1. **Separate trading-loss and custody-loss budgets.** A 3% strategy drawdown target cannot coexist with an unqualified promise of capital protection while 40% or 100% sits at one failure domain. Inventory all spot, collateral, pending transfers and earn balances. Cap the dollars that may be permanently lost on a single venue, bridge or issuer. If a venue loses all assets, a stop order cannot help.
2. **An outage state is not a flattened state.** Replace “always able to flatten” with a tested state machine. Record last confirmed positions; cancel entries where possible; query fresh state; alert an independent monitor; allow only bounded risk-reducing action after reconciliation. Do not blindly duplicate a hedge when an order acknowledgement is missing.
3. **A dead executor cannot flatten itself.** Use an external watchdog plus venue-side mechanisms where actually supported. Cancel-on-disconnect normally cancels orders; it does not necessarily close positions. If the venue or chain is unavailable, alert and contain the exposure you can still control.
4. **ADL needs a bounded orphan-leg policy.** On an unexpected short reduction, inhibit entry and reconcile the remaining spot. Subject to fresh balances and market health, reduce the now-unhedged spot using a preapproved emergency policy. Do not wait for a Telegram approval while delta risk remains. Reopening the short without reconciling can create a second failure.
5. **Stablecoin stops require independent references.** A local venue discount may be an exchange problem rather than a global issuer depeg. Observe at least two independent liquid markets and direct issuer status where available. “Flatten” into the same failing stablecoin does not remove depeg exposure. Predefine permitted conversion and withdrawal routes and accept that they can fail.
6. **Collateral migration is not instantaneous.** Stress price moves while transfers and withdrawals are unavailable. Automatic topups are capped to already approved reserves and aggregate venue limits. Never rescue a position by silently moving additional treasury capital onto a deteriorating counterparty.
7. **Legal evidence expires.** Keep a dated admission packet with legal entity, country/product eligibility, permissions, exact token/contract IDs, trading rules, fee tier, withdrawal rules, collateral model and account/API evidence. Recheck before live activation and on notices or product changes.

Dynamic terms can differ by region or legal entity.

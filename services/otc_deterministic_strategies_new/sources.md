# Binary source search, checked9 November2026

Recommendation: retain the current verified IQ Option transport and use explicit event/contract replay as the strategy judge. None of the inspected external bots provides reproducible OTC accepted-contract evidence strong enough to adopt a strategy as profitable. The useful external method is per-contract expected profit/loss accounting combined with chronology and payout admission, rather than a new indicator voting library. The two policies here are original deterministic hypotheses and copy no external implementation.

Read-only GitHub API evidence, commit/tree responses and the exact fetched source text live under `/tmp/binary-training/strategy-new/evidence/sources/`. Those remote files were inspected as data and never executed, installed or imported. The source ledger includes SHA256 for each response/file.

The four projects have too little reproducible deployment/economic evidence to rank them by stars alone. Open-issue counts do not measure responsiveness; that evidence was not verified. Zero open issues is not evidence of maintainer support. The inspected sources are snapshots at the pinned default-branch commits, not claims about later branch changes.

[Trading Binary Options Using Expected Profit and Loss Metrics](https://www.mdpi.com/2227-9091/10/11/212) treats reward and risk through expected profit/loss and binary payoff structure. It focuses mainly on one-day trades; it does not validate a120-second OTC signal. This supports separating predictive information from payout-adjusted economics.

[High Frequency Lead/lag Relationships](https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.7103v2) studies tick-level leads in stocks/futures. Its next-midquote information does not establish profitable market orders after spread, and it says nothing about these two synthetic OTC feeds or a120-second accepted binary contract. Slot4 borrows the causal event-study question, not the paper's reported accuracy. Our existing spent cross-feed diagnostic was near chance; the frozen alternative tests a narrower burst/flat mechanism without tuning it on new results.

[Alex Trade Room's BB/RSI video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7FnISelv7Dg) has a searchable description with BB14/deviation2 and RSI14/75/25 on60-second contracts. The description attributes its displayed profit to Martingale. It supplies no full per-order ledger, causal source replay or comparable120-second evidence. The description was inspected; no claim is made to have watched or verified its complete trades. [A newer BB/RSI video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p78LItP1hW8) was found in search but its rule details and ledger were not verified, so it supplies no candidate parameters or economic evidence.

Models/training takeaway: keep outcome intervals and feature publication times explicit, fit transformations on training only, stop on early-only data, calibrate separately, freeze the payout-aware policy before diagnostics, preserve every failed variant and use future prospective practice contracts for confirmation. This project's already frozen logistic/CatBoost comparison implements that separation. More model families do not repair unsupported payouts, ambiguous entries or insufficient future coverage.
